# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **QA/Testing Specialists**: Collaborate on testability requirements and review test results
- **Product Managers**: Implement features and provide technical feasibility feedback
- **Project Managers**: Provide estimates and updates on progress and blockers

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Define requirements and gather technical feasibility input
- **Product Leads**: Receive strategic direction and escalation on prioritization conflicts
- **Sponsors**: Report on outcomes and business impact
- **QA/Testing Specialists**: Define and validate acceptance criteria

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Align on priorities and manage dependencies
- **Developers**: Track progress, identify blockers, and facilitate resolution
- **Sponsors**: Escalate critical risks and business-impacting issues
- **Product Leads**: Report on status and seek guidance on scope changes

---

## QA / Testing Specialists

### Role Summary
QA and Testing specialists validate feature quality, ensure acceptance criteria are met, and identify defects before release. They collaborate with developers and product managers to define testability requirements and create test plans.

### Responsibilities
- Design and execute test plans (unit, integration, end-to-end, performance)
- Validate acceptance criteria and Definition of Done
- Report and track defects through resolution
- Recommend test automation opportunities
- Participate in release readiness assessments

### Goals
- Ensure quality standards are maintained
- Reduce production defects and customer impact
- Enable fast, confident releases

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and review meetings
- Defect reports and test result summaries
- QA sign-off in PR reviews and release checklists

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test design and validate defect fixes
- **Product Managers**: Review acceptance criteria and validate feature behavior
- **Project Managers**: Report on quality metrics and release readiness
- **Security/Compliance Specialists**: Participate in security and compliance testing

---

## Product Leads

### Role Summary
Product Leads own the strategic direction of a product area or platform. They approve major decisions, serve as escalation points for Product Managers, and align product work with business strategy and customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charters and major scope decisions
- Serve as escalation point for prioritization conflicts
- Align product initiatives with business strategy
- Review and validate success metrics and outcomes
- Mentor and guide Product Managers

### Goals
- Ensure product strategy alignment
- Maximize long-term customer value and business impact
- Enable effective PM decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Weekly PM sync (as needed)
- Project approval gates and reviews
- Monthly strategic alignment meetings

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Provide strategic guidance and approve major decisions
- **Project Managers**: Review project charters and milestone approvals
- **Sponsors**: Align on business strategy and investment decisions
- **Developers**: Participate in design reviews for strategic initiatives

---

## Sponsors / Executive Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Sponsors are executive-level stakeholders who have business authority and investment in the project outcome. They make go/no-go decisions, allocate resources, and serve as the ultimate escalation point for major risks and conflicts.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and business case
- Make go/no-go decisions at key gates
- Provide resources and remove organizational blockers
- Receive escalations on critical risks and issues
- Validate final deliverables against business objectives

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Minimize organizational friction and resource conflicts
- Enable rapid resolution of high-impact issues

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Escalations on critical business risks
- Post-project reviews and retrospectives

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Receive escalations and make go/no-go decisions
- **Product Leads**: Align on business strategy and investment priorities
- **Product Managers**: Review outcomes and business impact
- **Security/Compliance Specialists**: Address security and compliance escalations

---

## Security / Compliance Specialists

### Role Summary
Security specialists ensure that projects meet security and compliance requirements. They review designs, participate in threat assessments, and provide guidance on secure development practices.

### Responsibilities
- Review designs for security and compliance risks
- Conduct threat assessments and security reviews
- Provide secure coding guidance and best practices
- Participate in incident response and investigations
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and internal standards

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and compliance violations
- Enable secure, compliant product development
- Support incident response and investigation

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and threat assessments
- Security incident escalations and response
- Quarterly compliance audits and risk assessments

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide secure coding guidance and participate in code reviews
- **Project Managers**: Escalate security risks and incidents
- **QA/Testing Specialists**: Participate in security and compliance testing
- **Sponsors**: Escalate critical security or compliance violations
- **Product Leads**: Review security requirements for strategic initiatives

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Cross-role interactions help teams understand communication patterns and collaboration expectations.
